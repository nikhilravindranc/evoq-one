## 1. Marketing approach

### Who we are talking to
A company decision maker who needs a CRM and is tired of legacy tools, spreadsheets or too many disconnected apps. They are not a CRM expert. They want to know three things: will my team actually use it, will it fit how we sell, and will it work with the rest of our business.

### The idea the page is built on
Most CRMs go stale because keeping them current feels like extra work. Reports built on stale records cannot be trusted, so managers stop looking. Our page argues the opposite: a CRM that fits the way a team already sells gets used, and a CRM that gets used gives reliable numbers.

Positioning line (internal, not for the page):
> EVOQ CRM keeps every lead, deal and follow up in step with how your team sells, and sits beside the rest of your EVOQ apps.

### Four messages, in this order
1. **Clarity.** You can see where every deal stands without asking anybody.
2. **Fit.** Stages, fields, modules, roles and dashboards are set up around your process, not ours.
3. **Follow through.** Tasks, meetings and reminders keep follow ups from slipping.
4. **Connected.** The CRM shares a login with your other EVOQ apps, so sales does not become another island.

### Where AI sits
A supporting thread, not the lead. Buyers expect an assistant, but most are wary of an app that does things on its own. So we mention EVI twice, in plain words: once in the hero visual, and once in a short section about control (your admin decides how much EVI does by itself). No "AI powered" headline, no stacked claims.

### How we prove it
We cannot cite customers or numbers yet, so proof is the product itself: real screens, real field names, real stages. The call to action is always a demo. Pricing is on request.

### Tone
Plain sentences, concrete nouns, short lines. No dashes, no exaggeration, no status tags, no disclaimers. Competitor test: a line stays only if it would sound wrong with another CRM's name on it.

### What competitors teach us
| They do | We do |
|---|---|
| Lead with AI and superlatives ("easiest", "#1") | Lead with clarity and fit, no superlatives |
| Prove with customer counts and badges | Prove with screens and a demo |
| Offer a free trial | Offer a demo and pricing on request |
| Hide a long feature list in rows | Show seven areas, five features each, the rest on request |
| Treat the CRM as a stand alone product | Show the CRM beside HR, billing, desk and projects |

## 2. Page copy: Home

### Meta
- **Title:** EVOQ CRM | Pipeline, contacts, tasks & reports
- **Description:** Track leads, deals, contacts and follow ups in a CRM that follows your sales process. Customize stages, fields and roles. Book a demo.

### Section A. Hero
- **Eyebrow:** EVOQ CRM
- **Headline (recommended):** Know where every deal stands.
- **Alternatives:** Your pipeline, always current. / A CRM shaped around how you sell.
- **Subhead:** Leads, contacts, accounts and deals sit in a pipeline your team can shape with its own stages, fields and roles. Tasks and reports keep it current.
- **Primary CTA:** Book a demo
- **Secondary CTA:** See what it does
- **Hero visual brief:** Kanban pipeline with deals across stages, a manager's card with "3 proposals waiting on a reply", and a small EVI prompt reading "What should I focus on this week?". Clean invented names.

### Section B. Start from your goal
- **Title:** What do you want your CRM to help with?
- **Chips, each with a line and a matching screen:**
  - **Find new leads:** Capture leads, assign an owner and see every status from New to Qualified.
  - **Keep records current:** Every contact and account carries its full history, so nobody starts from a blank page.
  - **Prepare for meetings:** Open a record and see the conversations, notes and open tasks before you dial in.
  - **Follow up on time:** Tasks with due dates, priorities and reminders. Overdue ones surface first.
  - **Forecast revenue:** Deal probability, pipeline by stage and revenue trend in a single dashboard.

### Section C. Pipeline & deals
- **Title:** See the whole pipeline. Open any deal.
- **Body:** Switch between list, grid and Kanban views of the same deals. Each stage shows its probability, each deal shows its owner, amount and close date. Filter, sort and choose your columns so every team member sees what matters to them.
- **Three callouts:**
  - **Stages that match your process.** Qualification, needs analysis, proposal and decision, or the stages you already use.
  - **Probability on every deal.** A clear view of which deals are likely and which are stalling.
  - **Lead to deal without retyping.** Convert a qualified lead and keep its history.

### Section D. Features by area
- **Title:** Everything a sales team touches each day.
- **Subhead:** Pick an area to see its top features. Open the full list when you need it.

**1. Contacts & Accounts**
*Keep every relationship in context.*
- Profiles for people and for companies
- Full activity history on every record
- Notes and conversation history in the same place
- An owner on every record
- Import & export your existing data

**2. Leads & Pipeline**
*Move opportunities forward.*
- Lead statuses from New to Qualified, Lost or Unqualified
- List, grid and Kanban views
- Deal stages with probability
- Automatic lead assignment
- Filters, columns and sorting on every list

**3. Quotes & Revenue**
*Turn deals into predictable revenue.*
- Generate quotes from the deal record
- Approval steps before a quote goes out
- Payment tracking against each deal
- Lead scoring and win probability
- Pairs with ConfigX when quotes need product configuration

**4. Reports & Forecasting**
*Numbers your managers can trust.*
- Dashboards with a link from every card to its source report
- Saved reports with tags
- Revenue trend and pipeline by stage
- Revenue forecast
- Team performance and conversion trends

**5. Tasks & Meetings**
*Follow ups that do not slip.*
- Tasks with type, priority and due date
- Link a task to a contact or a deal
- Meetings and reminders
- Calendar sync
- A daily view of tasks due, deals to follow up and meetings

**6. Campaigns & Outreach**
*Reach the right people at the right time.*
- Segment customers by behavior, history or engagement
- Email and SMS campaigns
- Open rate, conversion and engagement tracking
- A single dashboard for results across campaigns
- Works with EVOQ Campaigns

**7. Automation & Customization**
*Make the CRM fit your process.*
- Custom modules and fields
- Workflow automation for repeated steps
- Roles and permissions
- Customizable dashboards and themes
- Data backup

**Mobile access** (a closing row under the explorer)
- Open deals, update leads, manage tasks and send email from your phone, including offline.

### Section E. Reports for every level
- **Title:** The same data, seen the way each person needs it.
- **Body:** A rep opens a day view. A manager opens pipeline by stage. An owner opens revenue trend. Every number links back to the report it came from, so questions get answered in a click.
- **Callout row:** Tasks due today · Deals to follow up · Meetings today · New leads this week

### Section F. Roles
- **Title:** Each role opens its own screens.
- **Sales rep & Account owner:** Start the day with tasks due, deals to follow up and meetings. Update a record in a few taps.
- **Sales manager:** See the pipeline by stage and by owner, review forecast and spot deals that have gone quiet.
- **Sales operations & Admin:** Shape modules, fields, roles and permissions. Import, export and back up data.
- **Leadership & Business owner:** Open the dashboard for revenue trend, booked revenue and pipeline health without asking for a report.

### Section G. Control
- **Title:** You decide who sees what, and what runs by itself.
- **Body:** Roles and permissions control access to modules, fields and records. Data import, export and backup sit in the same settings area. EVI, the EVOQ assistant, follows the same rules: your admin chooses whether it acts automatically, asks first, or only suggests.
- **Three cards:**
  - **Roles & Permissions.** Set who can view, edit or approve.
  - **Modules & Fields.** Add the fields your team actually uses.
  - **Import & Export.** Bring data in, take data out, keep a backup.

### Section H. EVI in your CRM (short)
- **Title:** Ask EVI what needs attention.
- **Body:** EVI can point out stalled deals and overdue follow ups, bring up customer history and draft a follow up. Work it prepares comes back to your team for review, based on the approval mode your admin has set.
- **CTA text link:** Explore EVOQ AI

### Section I. Works with EVOQ
- **Title:** Sales that connects to the rest of your business.
- **Body:** EVOQ CRM shares a login with your other EVOQ apps through EVOQ CTRL. Pair it with the apps your team already needs.
- **Cards (a title and a line each):**
  - **Campaigns.** Send outreach to the segments you build in the CRM.
  - **ConfigX.** Configure products and price quotes.
  - **Billing.** Invoice the deals you win.
  - **Desk.** Support tickets for the customers you sell to.
  - **Projects.** Plan the work that starts after the deal closes.

### Section J. Common questions
- **Can we use our own stages and fields?** Yes. Modules and fields are customizable, and pipeline stages can follow your process.
- **How do we bring in our existing records?** Use the import tool in settings. Export is available in the same place.
- **Who sees what?** Roles and permissions control access by user.
- **Does it work on a phone?** Yes. Deals, leads, tasks and email are available on mobile.
- **How much does it cost?** Pricing depends on your team and setup. Ask us during a demo.

### Section K. Closing
- **Title:** See it with your own pipeline.
- **Body:** Book a demo and we will walk through your stages, fields and roles in the CRM.
- **CTA:** Book a demo (opens the demo form)
- **Secondary:** Request pricing
