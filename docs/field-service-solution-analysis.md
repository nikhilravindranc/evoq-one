This analysis starts from the industries' needs and the products on the site, then proposes the solutions per area. Copy comes after you approve the solution list.

## 3. Proposed solutions per area

Nine solutions per panel, in the order the work moves, each tied to its product. Products in bold are ones the first two plans left out.

**Field service**
1. Service requests (Desk, ServiceOps)
2. **Online booking of service visits (Bookings, Appointments)**
3. Estimates and approvals (ServiceOps)
4. Scheduling and dispatch (ServiceOps)
5. Technician visits on mobile (ServiceOps)
6. Parts and stock (Inventory)
7. Invoices and payments (Billing)
8. **Customer accounts and follow-up (CRM)**
9. **Installations as projects (Projects)**

**Facilities management**
1. Site requests (Desk)
2. Work orders and inspections (ServiceOps)
3. Staff and contractor assignments (ServiceOps partners)
4. Assets and service history by site (ServiceOps)
5. **Room and equipment booking (Bookings, Spaces)**
6. **Amenity, court and venue booking (Bookings, Facilities)**
7. Stock and supplies (Inventory)
8. Invoices for client sites (Billing)
9. **Refurbishments and fit-outs (Projects)**

**Equipment and asset maintenance**
1. Asset records and warranty (ServiceOps)
2. Preventive schedules and service plans (ServiceOps)
3. Corrective repairs (ServiceOps)
4. Parts and stock (Inventory)
5. Job costs and margin (ServiceOps, Billing)
6. **Technician training and certifications (HRMS, Skillberry)**
7. **Maintenance reminders to customers (Campaigns, ServiceOps notifications)**
8. **Installations and upgrades as projects (Projects)**
9. Service history across sites (ServiceOps)

Under all three panels, two bands: **Sync** (accounting and other systems, where supported integrations exist) and **EVOQ AI** (EVI and the agents that draft and flag, while a person decides).
