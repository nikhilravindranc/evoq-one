What is EVOQ Sync?

EVOQ Sync is an enterprise-grade iPaaS built for teams that have outgrown one-off automations, scripts, and spreadsheet workarounds. It connects your CRM, ERP, eCommerce, accounting, and support systems into one stable integration backbone. You define the workflow and business rules, and we design, configure, and manage the integration for you. Sync enables secure bi-directional data flows, clear source-of-truth control, and full activity tracking through a simple dashboard. Your team stays focused on operations, not APIs, error logs, or maintenance.

As your business grows, new apps and changes plug into the same structured system, instead of creating yet another sync workflow.
