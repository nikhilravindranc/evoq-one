Built For Everyone Who Keeps Work Moving

Different responsibilities require different experiences. EVOQ HRMS provides dedicated workspaces for everyone involved in managing your workforce.

Employees

Access personal information, update profiles, request leave, download payslips, upload documents, and stay informed through a self-service experience designed for everyday use.

Managers

Leadership

Integrations

Works With The Systems You Already Use

Your HR processes shouldn't require replacing the tools your business already depends on.

EVOQ HRMS works as a standalone solution or connects with your existing productivity, communication, accounting, identity, and business applications. When you're ready to streamline payroll, simply connect EVOQ Payroll to manage employee compensation, statutory compliance, and payslips without duplicating employee data or disrupting existing HR processes. As your business grows, EVOQ HRMS also integrates with the broader EVOQ ecosystem, helping connect people, operations, customer management, commerce, and field service through a unified platform.

Whether you're replacing spreadsheets, modernizing existing processes, or introducing a dedicated HR platform, EVOQ HRMS adapts to your business without disrupting the way your teams already work.
